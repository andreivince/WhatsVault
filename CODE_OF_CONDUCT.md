# Code of Conduct

WhatsVault is a privacy-sensitive open-source project. Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to keep discussions respectful, focused, and safe for people who may be handling private chat archives.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Assume privacy reports are sensitive, even when details are incomplete.
- Use synthetic fixtures or redacted examples instead of private chats, backups, media, paths, contact names, phone numbers, or identifiers.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the code, docs, and user experience.
- Help new contributors find the right project boundary before asking them for private data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Posting private chat content, media, backup identifiers, local paths, device identifiers, contact details, or raw proof dumps.
- Pressuring a user or contributor to share private backup material publicly.
- Repeated off-topic comments that make issues or pull requests harder to maintain.

## Reporting

For security or privacy-sensitive issues, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For conduct concerns that are not security-sensitive, open a GitHub issue with only the minimum public-safe context needed to describe the problem.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments that expose private data or violate this code. Maintainers may also close issues or block accounts when behavior creates risk for users or contributors.
